# meiseki-growth-scout

A Claude skill: daily business-development and growth-intelligence research workflow for Meiseki, a Business Strategy / Growth Intelligence / Business Transformation consultancy that engages as a fractional CGO, CMO, Strategy Partner, Operations Partner, or Executive Advisor.

Finds a small number of high-conviction opportunities per run — not long prospect lists — split across direct client prospects (50%), referral partners (30%), and trigger-event opportunities (20%), filtered hard on verifiable buying signals and scored on urgency and budget fit.

## Files

- `SKILL.md` — the main workflow. Start here.
- `icp-and-signals.md` — ideal company profile, buying-signal list, problem-to-signal mapping, and the urgency/budget scoring rubric.
- `output-template.md` — the exact per-opportunity field list and daily report structure.

## How to use

Upload all three files in this directory (keep them together, flat, no nested subfolder) as a skill in Claude, or point Claude at this directory directly. Works alongside the Apollo.io and Clay MCP connectors if available in the session — Clay for sourcing/enrichment, Apollo for logging opportunities and driving outreach (see `SKILL.md` Section 6).
